Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

This policy describes the current planned handling of information. It will be updated if the service or data practices change.

This Privacy Policy explains how Overlays (“Overlays,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and deletes information when you use our application and website.

Information we collect

When you connect Strava, we receive information you authorize Strava to provide. This may include your Strava athlete identifier, profile information, activity details, routes, timestamps, distance, duration, speed, elevation, heart rate, power, cadence, and other recorded activity metrics. The information available depends on your activity, devices, sensors, privacy settings, and the permissions you grant.

We may also process photos or videos you select, overlay preferences, support requests, and limited technical information required to operate and secure the service.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate your account, show your own activities to you, create the photo or video overlays you request, provide support, protect the service, comply with legal obligations, and improve reliability. We do not sell your personal information.

Strava data

We access only data authorized by the authenticated athlete and use it only to provide the registered Overlays application. We respect Strava privacy settings and applicable Strava API requirements. Your use of Strava is also governed by Strava’s own terms and privacy policy.

Media and sharing

You choose the photos and videos processed by Overlays. You also choose whether to download or share a finished output. We do not publicly post your content without an action initiated by you.

Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide the service, meet security and legal requirements, and honor your choices. Temporary activity and media files are deleted when they are no longer needed for processing, subject to legitimate legal retention requirements.

Disconnecting Strava and deletion

You may revoke Overlays access through your Strava account settings. You may also request deletion of information associated with your Overlays account. See our Data Deletion Instructions. We will confirm completion and process valid requests within the period required by applicable law and Strava’s requirements.

Service providers

We may use vetted service providers for hosting, storage, security, analytics, customer support, and media processing. They may process information only to provide services to us and under appropriate contractual protections.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, object to, or delete your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Contact us to exercise an applicable right.

Children

Overlays is not directed to children under the minimum age required to consent to data processing in their jurisdiction.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will publish the revised policy and update the date above. We will provide additional notice when legally required.
