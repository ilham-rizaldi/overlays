Data Deletion

Last updated: August 28, 2026

You can disconnect Strava and request deletion of information associated with your Overlays account at any time.

Disconnect Overlays from Strava

  1. Sign in to your Strava account.
  2. Open Settings and select “My Apps.”
  3. Find Overlays and select “Revoke Access.”

Revoking access prevents Overlays from making future requests for your Strava data. We will also process the associated deauthorization event in accordance with Strava’s requirements.

Request deletion from Overlays

Send a request from the email address associated with your account to rizael.loo@gmail.com. Use the subject “Delete my Overlays data” and include enough information for us to verify and locate your account without sending passwords, access tokens, or sensitive activity details.

What we delete

Following verification, we will delete personal information under our control associated with your account, including retained Strava-derived data, authentication credentials, saved projects, uploaded media, and generated outputs, unless applicable law requires a limited record to be retained.

Confirmation and timing

We will acknowledge your request, complete deletion within the period required by applicable law and Strava’s requirements, and send written confirmation when deletion is complete.

Need help?

Contact rizael.loo@gmail.com.
